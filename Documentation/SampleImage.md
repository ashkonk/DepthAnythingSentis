A simple wooden stool sits farther behind to the right, with a large leafy plant farther back, a concrete floor with strong perspective, tall windows and repeating wooden beams receding into the distance. Natural diffuse daylight, realistic colors and textures, crisp details throughout the scene, moderate wide angle 35mm equivalent, no shallow depth of field, no extreme distortion. Clear separation of person from background for a person segmentation demo and several distinct depth layers. No text, logos, borders, watermark or interface.
